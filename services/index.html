Our Services

Two kinds of problems. One firm that handles both.

Every engagement starts with understanding what you're actually trying to accomplish — not just what you've asked us to build.

01

Salesforce Implementation

For companies building on Salesforce for the first time — or rebuilding what wasn't done right.

You had a vision for how your business should operate. The implementation didn't deliver it. Or you're starting fresh and want it done properly the first time. We design and build Salesforce around your process — not the other way around.

What we deliver
  • →Sales Cloud and Service Cloud implementation
  • →Lead to opportunity to contract process design
  • →Data migration and system integration
  • →User training and adoption support

When to engage: You're implementing for the first time, replacing a legacy CRM, or inheriting an org that doesn't reflect how your business works.

02

Revenue Visibility & Reporting

For leadership that has Salesforce but still can't see what's driving the business.

The data is there. The dashboards exist. But nobody trusts the numbers and leadership is still making decisions on gut feel and spreadsheet exports. We build the reporting infrastructure that turns your Salesforce into a system leadership actually relies on.

What we deliver
  • →Executive dashboard and KPI design
  • →Pipeline and forecast visibility
  • →Proactive alerts for deal risk and blockers
  • →Reporting your leadership can present upward

When to engage: Your forecast feels like a guess. Leadership wants visibility but the data isn't structured to provide it.

03

Salesforce DevOps

For teams that need to move fast without breaking things.

Changes going directly to production. No version control. No deployment process. One wrong move and something breaks for the entire team. We implement Copado-based DevOps so your Salesforce org has the same rigour as any serious engineering environment.

What we deliver
  • →Copado DevOps implementation
  • →Environment strategy and release management
  • →Version control and deployment automation
  • →Change governance and audit trail

When to engage: Your team is making untracked changes in production, or you're scaling and need process before things break.

04

Advisory & Architecture

For executives making a significant Salesforce decision and wanting an independent perspective before committing.

Before you spend the budget, know what you're buying. We provide honest, independent guidance on what to build, how to build it, and what to avoid — with no interest in selling you more than you need.

What we deliver
  • →Current state assessment and gap analysis
  • →Solution architecture and roadmap
  • →Build vs buy evaluation
  • →Pre-implementation planning and scoping

When to engage: You're about to make a major Salesforce investment and want clarity before you commit.
