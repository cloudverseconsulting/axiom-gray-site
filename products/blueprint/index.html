Privacy

Read-only. Local-first. Nothing leaves your org.

Blueprint reads Salesforce metadata — object names, fields, and relationships — to render architecture diagrams. It does not modify records or metadata, and does not upload Salesforce data to Axiom Gray or any third party.

No backend server

Blueprint has no backend. All requests go directly from your browser to your Salesforce org.

No separate login

Blueprint uses the active Salesforce browser session. No OAuth flow, no credentials stored.

Local storage only

Saved views, recent objects, and preferences are stored in Chrome local storage on your device.

Read-only access

FAQ

Common questions.

Does Blueprint require a separate Salesforce login?

No. Blueprint uses the active Salesforce browser session. As long as you are logged into Salesforce in Chrome, Blueprint connects automatically.

Does Blueprint modify Salesforce data?

No. Blueprint is strictly read-only and uses Salesforce metadata APIs. It does not modify records, metadata, permissions, or any configuration.

Does Blueprint work with custom objects?

Yes. Blueprint supports both standard and custom Salesforce objects. You can filter the graph to show standard only, custom only, or both.

Can I export diagrams?

Yes. Blueprint supports PNG, JPEG, SVG, and PDF exports. Use the Download button in the extension header once your graph is arranged.

Does Blueprint upload Salesforce data?

No. Blueprint is local-first and does not upload Salesforce metadata, saved views, or any org data to Axiom Gray or third parties.
