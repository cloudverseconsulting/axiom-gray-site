Fieldwise  /  Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

Overview

Fieldwise is a browser extension developed by Axiom Gray. This policy explains what data Fieldwise accesses, how it is used, and what we do not do with it.

Data We Access

Fieldwise reads your Salesforce session cookie (sid) scoped to *.salesforce.com domains. This cookie is used solely to authenticate Tooling API calls made directly from your browser to your Salesforce org.

Data We Do Not Collect

Fieldwise does not collect, store, transmit, or share any user data. Specifically:

  • — No data is sent to Axiom Gray's servers or any third party
  • — Your Salesforce session token is never logged or stored outside your browser
  • — FLS snapshots and apply history are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage and never leave your device
  • — We do not track usage, analytics, or any personally identifiable information

How Data Stays Local

All API calls made by Fieldwise go directly from your browser to your Salesforce org. Axiom Gray has no servers in this data path and no visibility into your Salesforce data.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and post the revised policy at this URL.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact us at contact@axiomgray.com.
