Privacy Policy

Your data stays in your browser.

FieldWise is built with a privacy-first architecture. Here is exactly how it works:

What we collect: Nothing.

FieldWise does not collect, store, or transmit any data. No analytics. No tracking. No account required.

How it works

FieldWise connects directly to your Salesforce org via the Salesforce Metadata API using your active browser session. All data is processed locally in your browser and never sent to any external server.

Data storage

FieldWise does not store any Salesforce data, field configurations, profile information, or personal data anywhere outside your browser session. When you close the extension, nothing is retained.

Third parties

FieldWise does not share any information with third parties. There are no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking tools embedded in this extension.

Permissions

FieldWise requests access to salesforce.com domains only, solely to read and write field level security configurations via the Metadata API on your behalf.
