About Axiom Gray

Built by someone who has done the work.

The Firm

The firm behind the firm.

Axiom Gray was founded by a Salesforce Solution Architect with over a decade of hands-on delivery experience across enterprise CRM implementations, legacy platform migrations, and Salesforce DevOps.

Before founding Axiom Gray, our founder led engagements across financial services, software, and life sciences — working directly with enterprise clients to define problems, architect solutions, and deliver production systems on Salesforce. That experience spans the full consulting lifecycle: from discovery and business analysis through to platform engineering, DevOps implementation, and client relationship management.

Axiom Gray was built on a simple observation: most Salesforce consulting firms are either too large to care about mid-market clients or too junior to handle architectural complexity. We sit in neither category. We bring enterprise-grade thinking to organizations that need it — without the overhead of a global SI.

Our Focus

Salesforce — End to End

Implementation, DevOps, solution architecture, and reporting. One platform. Delivered with depth.

Problems Worth Solving

We work with executives who have a real problem or a real idea — and the budget and intent to do something about it.

Senior Involvement. Always.

Every engagement is run by our founder. You will never be passed to someone who wasn't in the room when we scoped the work.

Differentiators

What makes us different.

Salesforce Depth Without the Overhead

We are not a generalist technology firm that happens to do Salesforce. Salesforce is our platform. We know it at the architectural level — data models, process design, deployment strategy, and reporting infrastructure — not just the configuration surface.

Engineering Discipline

Most Salesforce implementations have no deployment process, no version control, and no record of what was built or why. We treat every org like a production engineering environment — because what gets decided in month one determines what's possible in year three.

Founder Involvement on Every Engagement

You will not be handed to a junior team after the sales process. Our founder is involved in every engagement — from scoping through delivery. That is a deliberate choice, not a limitation.
